# Abdominal Myomectomy Procedure

## Pre-operative Care

1. Counsel the patient and obtain informed consent.
    
2. Perform necessary investigations:
    
    - Packed cell volume (PCV)
        
    - Grouping and cross-matching (GXM) for 2 units of blood
        
3. Admit the patient and inform the anesthetist.
    
4. Pre-operative preparations:
    
    - Keep patient nil per os (NPO) from midnight
        
    - Bowel preparation if indicated
        
    - Pass urethral catheter for bladder drainage
        
    - Treat any genital tract infection before surgery
        

---

## Intra-operative Procedure

### Preparation

1. Administer anesthesia:
    
    - General anesthesia or
        
    - Regional anesthesia
        
2. Position the patient in the supine position.
    
3. Perform surgical scrub and gowning.
    
4. Clean and drape the abdomen.
    

### Surgical Steps

5. Make an abdominal incision and deepen it into the peritoneal cavity.
    
6. Expose the operative field:
    
    - Balfour retractor → retract abdominal wall
        
    - Mild Trendelenburg position → displace bowel upward
        
    - Doyen’s retractor → retract and protect the bladder
        
7. Deliver the uterus and apply a uterine tourniquet:
    
    - Bonney’s myomectomy clamp or
        
    - Improvised Foley catheter (20–24 Fr)
        
8. Make an incision over the myoma on the uterus.
    
9. Enucleate (shell out) the myoma.
    
10. Close the dead space in layers to prevent hematoma formation.
    
11. Appose the serosa using a continuous baseball running suture with Vicryl 2/0.
    
12. Remove the tourniquet and secure hemostasis.
    
13. Close the abdomen in layers.
    
14. Clean and dress the wound.
    

---

## Post-operative Care

1. Transfer the patient to the recovery room.
    
2. Monitor vital signs, urine output, and bleeding.
    
3. Transfer the patient to the ward once stable.
