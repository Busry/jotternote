# DEFINITION

Reduction in hemoglobin concentration below  normal for age , sex  and gestational age.

For first and third gestational age hemoglobin concentration less than 11g/dl while in second trimester hemoglobin concentration less than 10.5g/dl.

# CLASSIFICATION

**Base on severity**

**Mild :** Hemoglobin concentration between 10.0 - 10.9 g/dL

**Moderate :** Hemoglobin concentration between 7.0 - 9.9 g/dL

**Severe :** Hemoglobin concentration < 7.0 g/dL


**Base on red cell indices(Morphological)**

**Microcytic, Hypochromic** : MCV < 80fL and MCH < 27pg
Eg. Iron deficiency, Thalasemia, Anemia of chronic disease, Lead poisoning, sideroblastic anemia

**Normocytic, Normocromic  :** MCV < 80-95 fL and MCH >= 27pg
Eg Hemolytic anemia, Anemia of chronic disease, Acute hemorrhage, Renal disease, Bone marow failure, mixed deficiencies.

**Macrocytic,**  : MCV > 95fL
Megaloblastic: vit B12 or folate deficiency
Non-Megaloblastic: Alcohol, liver disease, Myelodysplasia, Aplastic anemia

# ETIOLOGY

**Nutritional**
Eg iron , folate or bitamin B12 deficiency

**Hemorrhage**
Acute: APH, intrapartum, PPH
Chronic: Hookworm infestation, Bleeding, Hemorrhoid, PUD

**Hemolytic**
Inherited: Hemoglobinopathy, red cell membrane defects, Enzyme deficiency(G6PD)
Acquired: Malaria, HIV, Immune and non-immune hemolytic anemia

**Bone Marrow failure:**
I.e Aplastic anemia, Drugs(Zidovudin, Chloramphenicol

**Anemia of chronic disease**
**Infectious:** Pulmonary  abscess, Tuberculosis, Pneumonia,Osteomyelitis, bacterial endocarditis
**Non-infectious:** Rheumatoid arthritis, Connective tissue disease(SLE), Sarcoidosis, Crohns disease, Gauchers disease
**Malignancy:** carcinoma, sarcoma, lymphoma

# INVESTIGATIONS

FBC(Hb, PCV, MCV, MCH)

Blood film (Thin and Thick)

Reticulocyte count

Bone marrow studies

Hb electrophoresis

Parasite studies(MPs, stool for ova and parasites)

Iron studies(serum ferritin, TIBC, erythrocyte protoporphyrin, transferrin saturation)

Etiology specific: Urine mcs, EUCr, blood culture, coombs test

# TREATMENT

It is multidisciplinary, depend on etiology, severity and gestational age.
The goal is to treat the underlying cause, correct anemia,and prevent further complication and morbidity.

**Iron deficiency:**

Elemental iron 60mg B.D

Vitamin C 200mg daily

Folic acid 5mg daily

**Folate deficiency:**

Folic acid 5mg daily

**Pernicious anemia**

IM vitamin B12 1mg daily x 1/52, then  1mg weekly x 4/52 then  1mg monthly throughout the pregnancy

**Malaria:**

Quinine (in first trimester) , ACT

**Infection:**

Appropriate antibiotics

**Hookworm:**

Aldendazole, Mebendazole

# COMPLICATIONS

**Maternal**

a. Preterm labour

b. PPH

c. Postpartum depression

d. Reduce maternal infant bonding

e. Increase risk of intervention(c/s)

**Stillborn**
